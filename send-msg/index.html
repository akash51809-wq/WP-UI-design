How to Use

Follow these steps to send messages quickly and safely.

01

Choose WhatsApp

Select your connected WhatsApp account from the dropdown.

02

Type Number

Enter a 10-digit mobile number. Add commas to send to multiple numbers.

03

Upload Excel & Sample

Use Sample Excel to check the format, then upload Excel or CSV. The system reads the Number column automatically.

04

Remove Duplicates

Remove repeated and invalid numbers before sending so only clean, unique numbers remain.

05

Message, Emoji & Attachment

Write your message, add emojis with Emoji, and attach photos, PDFs, or documents when needed.

06

Voice Message

Use Send as Voice to convert your message into an MP3 voice note. Choose language in Voice Options and preview before sending.

07

Send Message

Click Send Message or Send as Voice to start 1-by-1 delivery with a controlled sending interval.

◆
Safety TipUse valid 10-digit Indian mobile numbers and follow WhatsApp messaging rules when sending bulk messages.
